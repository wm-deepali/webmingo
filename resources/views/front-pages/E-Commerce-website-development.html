CHOOSE YOUR COMMERCE MODEL

Types Of E-Commerce Websites We Develop.

Different commerce models need different store structures, features and customer journeys.

Single-Vendor E-Commerce

A dedicated online store for one brand — full control over catalogue, pricing and the entire customer journey.

Multi-Vendor Marketplace

A platform where multiple sellers manage products, with vendor dashboards, commissions and shared checkout.

B2B E-Commerce

Built for businesses selling to other businesses with bulk ordering, trade pricing and account-based purchasing.

B2C E-Commerce

Direct-to-consumer stores where customers browse, add to cart and purchase — ideal for retail and D2C brands.

B2B2C E-Commerce

A hybrid model where a business sells through another business to reach the end consumer.
