Questions About E-Learning & LMS Development?

Clear answers to common questions about building a learning platform with Web Mingo.

Can you build a completely custom LMS for our institute?

+

Yes. We can design and develop a custom LMS around your courses, students, faculty, assessments, workflows and business requirements instead of forcing your organisation into a fixed platform structure.

Can you develop an LMS for coaching and test preparation businesses?

+

Yes. The LMS can support courses, batches, test series, question banks, results, rankings, study material, faculty workflows and other education-specific requirements.

What is the difference between a single-tenant and multi-tenant LMS?

+

A single-tenant LMS is built for one organisation, while a multi-tenant or white-label LMS allows multiple institutes to operate independently within one centrally managed platform.

Can the LMS integrate with our CRM, ERP and other systems?

+

Yes. CRM, ERP, payment gateways, video platforms and other third-party services can be connected through available APIs or custom integration workflows based on your requirements.

Can you build Android and iOS apps along with the LMS?

+

Yes. Android and iOS applications can be developed alongside the LMS to provide students, faculty and administrators with a connected mobile learning experience.
