How Your Classified Portal Can Generate Revenue.

A classified platform isn't limited to one income stream — build in multiple monetization models that work together to maximize your earnings.

01

Free & Paid Listings

Let users post basic listings for free to drive volume, while charging for extended visibility, longer duration or extra listing slots.

Pay-per-listing
02

Featured Ads

Sellers pay to have their listing highlighted with a badge, color or border — boosting clicks and standing out from regular listings.

One-time fee
03

Premium Placement

Offer top-of-search, homepage or category-top positions so sellers can pay to be seen first by every visitor searching that category.

Position-based pricing
04

Seller Packages

Bundle multiple listings, featured slots and validity days into tiered packages — Starter, Growth, Business — priced for every type of seller.

Bundled pricing
05

Subscriptions

Turn regular sellers, dealers and businesses into recurring revenue with monthly or yearly plans for unlimited or higher-volume posting.

Recurring revenue
06

Banner Advertising

Sell homepage, category and sidebar banner space to brands and local businesses looking to reach your platform's audience.

Ad space rental

Questions About Classified Portal Development?

Clear answers to common questions about building and scaling a classified marketplace with Web Mingo.

Can you build a completely custom classified portal?

+

Yes. We can develop a custom classified portal around your categories, listing structure, seller workflows, search requirements, monetisation model and business rules.

Can users create and manage their own listings?

+

Yes. Sellers can create, edit, manage, renew and monitor their listings through dedicated seller tools based on the workflow defined for your marketplace.

Can you build advanced search and filtering?

+

Yes. Search and filters can be tailored to your categories with options such as location, price, condition, attributes, availability and other marketplace-specific requirements.

Can you integrate online payments and paid listings?

+

Yes. Payment gateways can support paid listings, featured listings, seller packages, subscriptions and other monetisation models required by your classified marketplace.

Can administrators manage and moderate listings and sellers?

+

Yes. A custom admin panel can provide listing approval, seller management, verification, reporting, category controls, moderation and other tools for managing the marketplace.
