FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions About Digital Branding?

From brand strategy and identity to digital design and brand consistency, here are answers to common questions about our digital branding services.

What does digital branding include?

+

Digital branding can include brand strategy, positioning, visual identity, logo design, typography, colours, brand guidelines and digital brand applications.

Can you create a brand identity from scratch?

+

Yes. We can develop your brand from the initial strategy and positioning through logo design, visual identity and digital brand applications.

Can you redesign my existing brand?

+

Yes. We can review your existing identity and create a refreshed brand direction while retaining the elements that are important to your business.

Can branding include my website and social media?

+

Yes. Your brand identity can be applied consistently across your website, social media, campaigns and other important digital touchpoints.

Do you provide brand guidelines?

+

Yes. Brand guidelines can define how your logo, colours, typography, imagery and other identity elements should be used across digital channels.
