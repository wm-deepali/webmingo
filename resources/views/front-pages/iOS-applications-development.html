Frequently Asked Questions About iOS App Development.

Straight answers to what most people ask before getting started.

How long does it take to develop an iOS application?

+

Timeline depends on the app's complexity, number of features, integrations, design requirements, testing, and backend development. We provide a realistic timeline once your requirements are understood.

How much does iOS app development cost?

+

Cost depends on app complexity, number of screens, custom features, backend requirements, API integrations, payment and notification functionality, and testing needs. We recommend requesting a project-specific estimate.

Do you develop native iOS applications?

+

Yes, iOS applications are developed using native technologies such as Swift, with the specific approach selected according to the project's requirements.

Can you integrate Apple Pay, APIs, maps, and OTP?

+

Required third-party integrations can be planned and implemented based on technical compatibility, business needs, and provider requirements — including Apple Pay and mapping services where relevant, subject to regional and provider availability.

What is the iOS App Store approval process?

+

iOS applications submitted to the App Store must follow Apple's applicable review guidelines and submission requirements. This includes app configuration and release preparation, required app information and metadata, compliance checks, testing before submission, and review by Apple — which may include feedback requiring updates or corrections. We cannot guarantee approval or a fixed review timeline, as requirements can vary depending on the app and Apple's current policies.

Can my iOS app support offline functionality?

+

Selected offline features may be supported through local data handling and synchronization, depending on the application's workflow and technical requirements.

Should I choose Android, iOS, or Flutter?

+

Choose Android when your primary audience uses Android devices or your distribution strategy focuses on the Google Play ecosystem. Choose iOS when your product specifically targets Apple users and the App Store ecosystem — that's what this page covers. Consider Flutter when you want Android and iOS applications from a shared codebase and your project is suitable for a cross-platform approach.
